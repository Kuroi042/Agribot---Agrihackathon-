# Comma-separated fault codes. Use NONE when there are no device errors.
# Supported codes and suggested first checks:
# LOW_SOLAR           - Check panel shading, soiling, strings, and DC connections.
# OVER_TEMPERATURE    - Stop if necessary; allow cooling and check ventilation/fans.
# HIGH_CURRENT/OVER_CURRENT - Reduce load; inspect the pump, cable, and motor before restart.
# LOW_CURRENT         - Stop and check for dry running, a loose cable, or a disconnected pump.
# LOW_BATTERY         - Reduce demand and check battery state, charging, and terminals.
# LOW_WATER           - Stop the pump and restore a safe source-water level.
# DRY_RUN             - Stop the pump; prime it and inspect the suction line and source level.
# OVERLOAD            - Stop the pump and inspect for a jammed pump or excessive load.
# OVER_VOLTAGE        - Isolate the supply and have DC/AC supply settings checked.
# UNDER_VOLTAGE       - Check the supply, battery, PV strings, and cable connections.
# PHASE_LOSS          - Stop operation and have the input phases and wiring checked.
# GROUND_FAULT        - Isolate power and have insulation/cabling checked by a qualified technician.
# COMMUNICATION_FAILURE - Check controller, network, and communication wiring.
# MOTOR_OVERHEAT      - Stop the motor; let it cool and inspect loading and ventilation.
# PUMP_BLOCKED        - Stop and isolate power before clearing a blockage.
# SENSOR_FAILURE      - Inspect the affected sensor and its wiring; replace if needed.
